## 1. Purpose of the study

The study investigates how people perceive the similarity of short videos of natural scenes and materials (e.g. water, vegetation in wind, fire, fabrics, distant traffic). Your responses will be compared with computational measures of video similarity. The results are used exclusively for basic research in computer vision and visual perception.

## 2. Procedure

- Participation takes place online in a web browser on a desktop or laptop computer (not on a mobile phone).
- One session takes approximately {{DURATION}} and consists of {{N_TRIALS}} short trials. {{BREAK_SENTENCE}}
- In each trial you will see three short looping videos: one at the top and two at the bottom. Your task is to choose which of the two lower videos is more similar to the upper one in its overall dynamic visual character. You may also answer "cannot decide".
- At the start you will provide three short items: an age band, whether you use vision correction (glasses/contact lenses), and the interface language.
- If you wish, you may take part in at most two sessions.

## 3. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing the browser window, without giving a reason and without any consequences. Incomplete sessions are not included in the main analysis.

After completing a session you will receive a **completion code**. Using this code you may request deletion of your responses until [TO BE COMPLETED BY AUTHORS: date, e.g. close of data collection] (see Section 6). Without the code your responses cannot be traced, because the data are pseudonymous.

## 4. Risks and discomfort

The study carries no risks beyond those of ordinary video viewing on a computer. Prolonged concentration may cause mild eye fatigue; for this reason a break is scheduled and the session can be interrupted at any time.

**Note on photosensitivity:** The videos contain no flicker or flashes, but they may contain fast motion (e.g. rippling water, leaves in wind, passing vehicles). If you have photosensitive epilepsy or find rapidly moving images uncomfortable, we recommend that you do not take part.

## 6. Personal data protection (GDPR)

- We **do not record** your name, e-mail address, IP address or any other direct identifier.
- On first launch your browser is assigned a random pseudonymous identifier (`observer_id`), used only to link the trials within a session and to ensure that you do not see the same video triplet twice.
- We store: your choices, response times, the order and identifiers of the videos shown, timestamps, technical playback information, screen resolution, browser type (user agent), and the three items listed above (age band, vision correction, language).
